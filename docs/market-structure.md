# Price structure and volatility

The price-detail panel adds closed-candle Wilder ATR, relative volume, confirmed swing/equal levels, structural breaks, and a break → retest → continuation study. These are independently implemented measurements inspired by the concepts referenced in [the LuxAlgo review](luxalgo-review.md); no external Pine source is included. Existing RSI, calendar sweep rules, and harmonic lecture templates are unchanged.

ATR (14) seeds from the mean of the first 14 true ranges, including high−low for the first candle, then uses Wilder smoothing. A price gap between consecutive candles belongs to true range. A missing candle, invalid OHLCV, changed interval, or interior unclosed candle restarts warmup. Trailing live candles cannot change the completed calculation. Relative volume divides the latest completed volume by the preceding 20 completed candles' mean; zero baselines and insufficient history are unavailable. Distance is shown both as a percentage of current price and in completed ATR units. Zero ATR is unavailable for normalization.

Strict 3/3 wick pivots appear only when the third right-hand candle closes. Equal-price plateaus and candles that are simultaneously strict highs and lows do not establish directional pivots. Nearby same-side pivots form equal-high/low clusters when their whole price range fits within 0.10 ATR at confirmation. The fallback before ATR is available is 0.05% of price. A cluster uses the highest high or lowest low as its boundary, appears when its second pivot confirms, and supersedes the displayed individual swing. Subsequent touches update its geometry and `lastConfirmedAt`; they are never available at the earlier pivot time. Levels expire after 120 candles without a newly confirmed member.

A strict wick breach marks a level swept. A strict close back on the original side separately marks a reclaim; equality and a breach alone do not qualify. The first sweep's penetration, wick/body proportions and relative volume remain available separately from a later reclaim candle's close-back measurement. These are OHLCV observations, not order-flow, absorption, or probability estimates.

A close crossing a confirmed high/low with a 0.05 ATR buffer produces a structural break. Same-bar crossings of several levels produce one event. A break in the initial or current direction is BOS; an opposite break is CHoCH. These labels describe this explicit local rule, rather than a universally standardized market-structure definition. Each level can generate one break. A new event starts a retest study using a frozen ±0.25 ATR band (0.05% fallback). The break candle cannot be its own retest. Retest must occur within the next 12 completed candles; continuation must close past the retest candle's extreme within the next eight candles. A wick through the far side of the retest band invalidates, and invalidation wins an ambiguous candle. Confirmation is a study event, not an executed order.

The mounted panel scopes its tracker to market, timeframe, and symbol. It retains earlier completed candles across overlapping rolling snapshots, replays overlapping corrections, and explicitly resets on disconnected histories or interior interruptions. Unchanged completed snapshots reuse the analysis, so live ticks do not replay the retained history. Session history is capped at 10,000 candles by default (expanded if needed for custom lifecycle lengths); crossing that limit retains half the window, recalculates ATR and structure, and visibly reports `historyResetReason: history-limit`. The retained suffix exceeds configured pivot, level, and retest lifetimes, but exact ATR continuity is not claimed across a cap reset. This continuity is in memory for the mounted panel; it does not claim continuity across a reload. The pure `analyzeMarketStructure` function analyzes the supplied contiguous completed suffix, and every event exposes its observable confirmation time.

Calendar details preserve the established approach-side/sweep/reclaim rules and add visible ATR penetration, close-back, wick/body, and volume measurements. Optional strict Bat diagnostics compare observed AB/XA (0.382–0.500), BC/AB (0.382–0.886), CD/BC (1.618–2.618), and AD/XA (0.886 ± 0.020). The latter two remain unavailable until an observed closed D touch; the projected zone midpoint is never substituted. Diagnostics do not alter lecture detection or invalidate its setup.
